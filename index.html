A seat for everyone

Plants on the plate.
Joy at the table.

Vegetarian food skips meat and fish. Vegan food takes it a step further, leaving out all animal-derived ingredients. Both are delicious ways to explore new flavors, cook with the seasons, and make everyday meals feel a little more intentional.

Whether you're just getting curious or already cooking this way, there's no one right path. Start with a meal you love and make it your own.

✿

Why go plant-forward?

Discover new ingredients, enjoy more colorful meals, and choose a way of eating that feels good for you and the planet.
